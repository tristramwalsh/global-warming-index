**Notes:** Model output, so the ERF and GMST pair only with each other, for the same SSP: never with observations or another model. The ERF is a single timeseries shared by all 50 GMST members.

**History and projection:** CMIP6 historical simulations to 2014, then each SSP from 2015. The GMST of each member is identical across the three SSPs up to 2014. The ERFs agree up to 2014 too, except that the CO2 ERF (and so the well-mixed greenhouse gas and anthropogenic ERF) differs between SSPs by up to 0.003 W/m² before 2015, although the CO2 concentrations are identical.
