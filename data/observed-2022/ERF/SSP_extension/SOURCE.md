**Dataset:** SSP-extended effective radiative forcing from FaIR v2.1.3, calibration v1.4.5

**From:** Supplied by Chris Smith, Feb 2025

**Notes:**
- Historical ERF to 2022, then projections along each SSP (ssp119, ssp126, ssp245, ssp370, ssp434, ssp460, ssp534-over, ssp585) to 2100. All SSPs are identical up to 2022 and diverge from 2023, so the dataset is filed with the 2022 data.
- Not the same historical ERFs as the IGCC ERFs (`data/observed-2022/ERF/IGCC/`). Both are ERF ensembles, but they are generated differently, so they differ before 2022 as well: SSP_extension-2022-<SSP> is not IGCC-2022 with an extension added.
