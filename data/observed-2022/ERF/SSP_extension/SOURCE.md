**Dataset:** SSP-extended effective radiative forcing from FaIR v2.1.3, calibration v1.4.5

**From:** Supplied by Chris Smith, Feb 2025

**Notes:** Historical ERF to 2022, then projections along each SSP (ssp119, ssp126, ssp245, ssp370, ssp434, ssp460, ssp534-over, ssp585) to 2100. All SSPs are identical up to 2022 and diverge from 2023, so the dataset is filed with the 2022 data. Its historical ERFs are calculated differently from the IGCC ERFs, so the two do not match.
